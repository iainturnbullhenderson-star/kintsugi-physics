About

I am a registered nurse and ophthalmic practitioner working independently on the geometric foundations of physics. I think in topological patterns rather than formal proofs. In September 2025 I had the intuition that the minimum stable configuration of a continuous spacetime field is a precessing tetrahedron — and that from it, without additional postulates, you can derive spin-½, electric charge, matter-antimatter asymmetry, and the rate of local time.

I have been developing that intuition into rigorous preprints, interactive web modules, and a book manuscript since then. This site is the open companion — the place where the geometry becomes visible to anyone.

The kintsugi metaphor is not decoration. Physics has cracks. The gold is in the cracks. That is where the new theories enter.
